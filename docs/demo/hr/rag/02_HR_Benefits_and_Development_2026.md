## 1. Medical plan

Permanent employees are enrolled in the company medical plan from their first
employment day. Coverage is subject to the insurer's terms and the benefit
schedule available in the secure HR Portal.

An employee may enroll one legal spouse or registered partner and eligible
dependent children within 30 calendar days of the employee's start date or a
qualifying life event. Late enrollment is considered at the next annual
enrollment window unless the insurer accepts an exception. Supporting
documents must be uploaded only through the secure dependent-enrollment case.

The HR knowledge base must not contain medical claims, diagnosis, treatment,
dependent identity numbers, or copies of personal certificates.

## 2. Wellness allowance

Each permanent employee may claim up to THB 12,000 per calendar year for
eligible wellness expenses. The allowance is not carried forward and is not
convertible to cash.

Eligible examples include fitness membership, organized sports fees,
ergonomic equipment for a primary workspace, and a recognized wellbeing
application subscription. Ineligible examples include ordinary clothing,
cosmetics, food, supplements, travel, gifts, and equipment purchased for
another person.

The employee submits an itemized receipt and proof of payment through HR Portal
→ Benefits → Wellness Claim within 60 calendar days of purchase. Total Rewards
reviews the claim. The stated service target is seven business days after a
complete submission. Reimbursement follows the next available payroll cycle.

## 3. Learning and professional development

Permanent employees receive a learning allowance of up to THB 30,000 per
calendar year, subject to available business-unit budget. The activity must be
relevant to the employee's current role or an agreed development plan.

### 3.1 Approval path

1. The employee submits the course title, provider, dates, cost, learning
   objective, and business relevance before making a purchase.
2. The direct manager approves requests up to THB 15,000.
3. Requests above THB 15,000 also require the business unit head.
4. Certification exams related to regulated duties additionally require the
   Compliance Learning coordinator.
5. HR Learning confirms the final approval in the HR Portal.

An employee must not assume reimbursement from a manager's chat message alone.
The HR Portal must show **Approved** before purchase. The service target for a
complete request is five business days.

### 3.2 Completion evidence

The employee uploads a completion certificate or attendance record within 30
calendar days after the activity. When a course is cancelled, the employee
must notify HR Learning and follow the provider's refund process.

## 4. Employee assistance programme

The Employee Assistance Programme provides confidential counselling through an
independent provider. Employees access it through the private link and phone
number in the secure HR Portal. Managers and HR do not receive individual case
content. In an emergency, employees should contact local emergency services;
the programme and this Knowledge document are not emergency services.

## 5. Benefit interpretation

The benefit amount, eligibility, and process in this guide are internal demo
policy. Tax treatment, insurer decisions, and statutory benefits may differ.
Total Rewards is the owner for benefit interpretation and exceptions.
