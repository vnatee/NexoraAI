# Asteria Financial Services — Employee Service Procedures 2026

> DEMO DOCUMENT — FICTIONAL DATA FOR NEXORAAI PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION ONLY.
> Document owner: HR Shared Services
> Version: 1.1
> Effective date: 15 February 2026
> Review date: 15 November 2026
> Service channel: HR Portal → Employee Services

## 1. Service principles

Employees should use the HR Portal for requests that contain personal data or
require tracking. General policy questions may be asked through the approved
HR assistant, but confidential documents must not be pasted into chat.

Service targets begin when all required information is received. A service
target is not a guarantee and does not represent approval.

## 2. Employment confirmation letter

Use **Employee Services → Letters → Employment Confirmation**.

Required information:

- language: Thai or English;
- purpose category: banking, visa, housing, or other;
- addressee, if a named addressee is required;
- whether base salary must be included; and
- requested delivery format: digitally signed PDF or physical original.

The standard service target is three business days for a digitally signed PDF
and seven business days for a physical original. Salary is excluded unless the
employee explicitly requests it through the secure form. The letter is sent
only to the employee's verified company account or collected using the secure
method stated in the case.

## 3. Personal-information change

Use **Employee Services → Personal Information → Change Request**. The employee
selects the field to change and uploads only the supporting document named by
the secure form. HR Shared Services validates the request within five business
days. A bank-account change received after the monthly payroll cutoff may take
effect in the following payroll cycle.

The standard monthly payroll cutoff is 15:00 Thailand time on the 15th calendar
day. If the 15th is a non-business day, the cutoff is 15:00 on the preceding
business day. Employees must confirm the effective cycle in the completed case.

## 4. New-starter onboarding

### 4.1 Before start date

- The hiring manager confirms role, cost centre, location, and system-access
  profile at least ten business days before the start date.
- HR Shared Services issues the onboarding checklist.
- IT prepares the approved standard equipment and accounts.
- The employee submits required identity and payroll documents only through the
  secure onboarding portal.

### 4.2 First week

- The manager completes the team induction and assigns mandatory learning.
- The employee completes Information Security, Code of Conduct, Data Privacy,
  and Workplace Safety learning within five business days.
- Access problems are reported through Service Desk; policy questions are
  reported through HR Portal.

The HR assistant may produce a checklist but cannot verify that an employee has
completed onboarding tasks unless an authorized system integration returns that
status.

## 5. Resignation and offboarding

An employee submits resignation through **Employee Services → Employment →
Resignation** and attaches the signed notice. HR Operations confirms the
contractual notice period and final working date. The manager must not promise
a shortened notice period before written approval.

The employee returns company assets no later than the final working day,
completes required handover, and submits outstanding expense claims before the
deadline shown in the offboarding case. IT disables access according to the
approved offboarding workflow. Final payroll timing is confirmed by Payroll in
the case because it may depend on cutoff, deductions, and applicable law.

## 6. Escalation matrix

| Topic | Owner | Escalation path | Standard target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leave policy or exception | HR Operations | HR Portal → Policy Question/Exception | 3 business days |
| Medical and wellness benefits | Total Rewards | HR Portal → Total Rewards | 7 business days |
| Learning request | HR Learning | HR Portal → Learning | 5 business days |
| Employment letter | HR Shared Services | HR Portal → Letters | 3 or 7 business days |
| Payroll or bank detail | Payroll Operations | Secure payroll case | 5 business days |
| System access | IT Service Desk | Service Desk ticket | Per IT priority |

## 7. Information the HR assistant must not request

The HR assistant must not request passwords, one-time codes, national ID
numbers, bank-account numbers, detailed medical information, disciplinary
records, performance ratings, compensation of another employee, or documents
belonging to another person. It should direct the employee to the secure HR
Portal when such information is required by a controlled process.
