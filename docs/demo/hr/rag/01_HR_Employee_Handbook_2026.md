## 1. Scope and policy hierarchy

This handbook applies to permanent employees of Asteria Financial Services in
Thailand. Fixed-term employees should refer to their employment agreement and
contact HR Operations when the agreement differs from this handbook.

Applicable law and a signed employment agreement take precedence over this
handbook. A later policy with a newer effective date takes precedence over an
older internal policy. Managers cannot create exceptions unless a policy names
them as an approving role. HR Operations must confirm any unresolved conflict.

## 2. Probation and employment status

The standard probation period is 119 calendar days from the employee's start
date. The employee's manager conducts a midpoint discussion by day 60 and a
final review no later than day 110. Completion of probation must be confirmed
in writing by HR Operations; the passage of time alone is not confirmation.

Employees on probation receive medical benefits from their first employment
day. Annual leave may be requested during probation only with manager approval;
the available balance is the prorated balance shown in the HR Portal.

## 3. Working hours and hybrid work

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00, including a one-hour
meal break. A business unit may define different coverage hours in writing.

Permanent employees who have completed 30 calendar days of employment may
request up to two remote-working days per week. The manager approves the normal
weekly pattern. Employees must attend designated office, client, audit,
regulatory, incident-response, or training sessions even on a usual remote day.

Remote work outside Thailand is not covered by ordinary hybrid-work approval.
It requires prior written approval from HR Operations, Information Security,
Tax, and the employee's business unit head. Employees must not work from an
unapproved country while waiting for a decision.

## 4. Annual leave

### 4.1 Entitlement

Permanent employees receive 12 working days of annual leave for each complete
calendar year. In the first employment year, the entitlement is prorated by
complete months of service. The HR Portal is the authoritative source for the
available balance.

### 4.2 Request and approval

- Requests for one or two consecutive working days should be submitted at least
  three calendar days before the first leave date.
- Requests for three or more consecutive working days should be submitted at
  least seven calendar days before the first leave date.
- The direct manager is the approving role.
- A request is not approved until the HR Portal shows status **Approved**.
- Urgent requests may be submitted with a reason, but are not automatically
  approved and remain subject to operational coverage.

### 4.3 Carry-forward

Up to five unused annual-leave days may carry into the next calendar year.
Carried days expire on 31 March of that next year. The HR Portal consumes
carried days before current-year days. Unused balance above the carry-forward
limit expires unless a written exception is issued by HR Operations.

## 5. Sick leave

Employees should notify their manager before the normal start time and record
sick leave in the HR Portal as soon as reasonably possible. A medical
certificate is required when sick leave exceeds two consecutive working days.
HR Operations may request supporting documentation where permitted, but the
employee should not upload detailed medical records unless specifically asked
through the secure HR case channel.

The employee should provide only the information required by the HR Portal.
Medical diagnosis and treatment details are confidential and must not be placed
in a general team chat, email distribution list, or Knowledge Data Group.

## 6. Parental and family-related leave

The demo company's primary-caregiver leave is 90 calendar days with full base
pay for permanent employees who have completed 12 months of service by the
expected start date. Secondary-caregiver leave is 15 working days with full
base pay and must be used within 90 calendar days of the relevant birth or
placement event.

Employees should open a confidential HR case at least 30 calendar days before
the expected start date where practicable. HR Operations confirms eligibility,
dates, documentation, statutory interaction, and payroll treatment. This
section describes fictional company policy and is not legal advice.

## 7. Policy exceptions and escalation

An employee or manager may request an exception through HR Portal → Employee
Services → Policy Exception. The request must identify the policy section,
business reason, requested dates, and risk-control proposal. HR Operations and
the designated business approver decide the request. Until written approval is
recorded, the published policy remains in effect.
